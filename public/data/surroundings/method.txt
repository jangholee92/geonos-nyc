NYC building surroundings: method and interpretation

Purpose
This derived snapshot helps a reader investigate the mapped ground near an individual building. It does not estimate indoor flood depth, safe access, damage, annual probability, or a winning site. The NYC Stormwater Resiliency Plan (May 2021), printed page 20 / PDF page index 19, describes buildings as voids in the 2D surface-water mesh. An interior-point non-hit is therefore not evidence of low flood risk.
https://www.nyc.gov/assets/orr/pdf/publications/stormwater-resiliency-plan.pdf

Coverage and source versions
Exactly 1,082,964 NYC OTI footprint records were reconciled against the full 5zhs-2jue snapshot updated 2026-10-25T08:21:07Z and retrieved 2026-10-27. They include 868,582 Building features and 213,408 Garage features, plus other source feature types. 1,082,923 valid, non-placeholder records were computed; 7 invalid geometries and 34 placeholders retain unknown values.
The stormwater polygons come from the official 9i7c-xyvv downloadable FileGDB archive, data timestamp 2024-07-03, metadata timestamp 2024-10-17, retrieved 2026-10-27. Both current-sea-level and 2050 moderate scenarios use 2.13 inches of rain in one hour. They are published scenarios, not observations of conditions in 2026. Future high tide is a distinct coastal category (+30 inches), not a stormwater depth class. Do not attribute a local map difference solely to sea-level rise without verifying the entire shared model baseline.
Future exclusion and wetland/waterbody masks come from the hosted 2050 scenario's layers 0, 3 and 2 respectively. Their absence does not prove complete model coverage. Do not silently apply these as definitive current-scenario coverage masks.

Geometry and measurements
All source polygon components are retained. There is no analytical simplification or area-based component removal. Geometries are projected to EPSG:2263, whose US survey foot is 1200/3937 meters. GEOS make_valid normalizes self-touching source rings; the largest total category-area change is less than 0.000001 square foot. The topology audit and original geometry checksums are retained.
The minimum outline-to-category distance is computed globally using full category geometry. There is no search-distance cap. Actual topological contact is stored separately because rounded distances can approach zero. A minimum distance is not a walkable route, entrance distance, or surveyed access condition.
Outward bands are buffer(5 m) minus footprint, buffer(10 m) minus buffer(5 m), and buffer(20 m) minus buffer(10 m). Buffers use 32 segments per quadrant, with a maximum circular approximation error of about 6 mm at 20 m. Bands include ground geometrically outside the footprint, including holes/courtyards where applicable; they may also include other structures or private land. These distances are explicit screening choices, not official risk thresholds. Each category is intersected and unioned separately. Categories are not summed into a risk score. Exclusions and water remain separate.
Areas in public shards are rounded to 0.1 m²; distances are stored to 0.001 m for reproducibility but should normally be displayed as whole meters or '<1m'. Computational precision does not establish source or survey accuracy. An area rounded to zero does not prove no tiny intersection; use the minimum distance and separate contact flags.

Source revision distinction
The current hosted ArcGIS service and the downloadable FileGDB have different geometries. For example, recomputed deep-class totals are 42,995,641 ft² hosted versus 40,126,225 ft² downloaded. These are separately identified official revisions, not interchangeable live results. The public surroundings snapshot consistently uses the downloadable geometry. Source metadata dates and checksums, including masks, are in manifest.json.

Validation
All 129 shard hashes and 1,082,964 unique IDs were checked. Every record is in the correct shard; all values are finite/nonnegative or explicitly unknown; the 41 unknown IDs match native quality flags. Across 114 footprints, Python representative points exactly match the app's TypeScript scanline algorithm and canonical SHA-256 fingerprints match JavaScript. Eight real-site minimum distances were independently checked against brute-force distance to every category component; differences are below 0.0005 m, consistent with storage rounding. Doubling buffer resolution changed sampled mapped band areas by at most 0.165 m². An analytic rectangle check, disjoint external-band checks, and live 2050 service patch checks also passed.

Independent stratified recomputation: 80 additional records (16 per borough) passed using original footprint CSV WKT and native DEP FileGDB polygons read directly from the official ZIP. The independent meter-coordinate path uses brute-force nearest distances and cumulative-buffer area subtraction. The sample includes 20 footprints with interior holes, 30 mapped-band hits, 23 mask/water-band hits and 36 all-band no-hits (categories overlap). Maximum discrepancies were 0.0004992 m for distance and 0.053780 m² for packaged band area, within the documented rounding. US survey-foot conversion was checked against CRS metadata and a one-square-meter analytic control. This is an 80-record numerical audit, not an independent citywide recomputation or physical model validation; it shares the GEOS and PROJ engines. Machine-readable results: /reports/surroundings_spatial_audit.json

Browser delivery and resource limits
The 129 deterministic .json.gz files total 73,727,636 stored bytes and decode to exactly 192,532,821 JSON bytes. The largest compressed shard is 643,063 bytes; the largest decoded shard is 1,694,262 bytes. Every decoded SHA-256 and record count matches the original JSON manifest. Metadata and method text remain uncompressed. The browser lazily fetches and decompresses only the selected building's shard and caches at most 12 shards. Assets remain outside JavaScript bundles; never load the entire archive into a 128 MiB Worker or eagerly fetch every shard. Original uncompressed JSON and research SQLite remain outside the site repository.
Each row carries the native source edit date, feature type and a SHA-256 fingerprint of geometry type and ordered coordinates rounded to 1e-7 degrees. A geometry/edit-date mismatch, missing row, invalid/placeholder shape, or fetch failure remains stale/unknown. It is never converted to a zero-valued result.

Reproduction
The research/decision_patterns pipeline contains source inventory and mask downloads, full outline audit, full surroundings computation, export, all-row export checks, independent validation, and Smith Street story extraction. Full surroundings computation took 693.8 seconds; all counts are recorded in the manifest. Existing source catalogs retain their counts: this is a derived analysis of existing sources, not an additional original dataset.
