How these videos were assembled

The application views are actual captured product states, arranged into an edited walkthrough rather than a continuous screen recording. Narration uses a synthetic English voice. Cut timing does not represent provider latency.

The Muse Image concept is an earlier, separately verified generated example for 572 Smith Street and 611 Court Street. It is not the result of the later 572/592 Smith Street production sequence, not a production UI screenshot, and not a successful retry of a refused request. The concept is an illustration, not site evidence.

Public records do not establish usable area, spare power, structural capacity, permission or investment return. SAM masks and DINOv2 differences guide inspection; they do not prove physical changes, safety or climate resilience. Some benign model requests remain subject to provider refusals.
