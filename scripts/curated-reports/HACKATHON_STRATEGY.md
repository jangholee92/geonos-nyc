# What would change your mind?

Product strategy and verified judging criteria · updated 28 October 2026 UTC

GEONOS starts with a recognizable property question, tests explicit conditions against compatible evidence, and identifies the missing fact worth checking next. Its creative idea is to make the boundary of evidence visible: the edge of a building, the scope of an energy report, and the timestamp of a weather observation. Each reveal should change a concrete next action.

This is submission preparation. The criteria are official; the product strategy below is our interpretation. It does not promise a prize or certify completed deployment or live Meta execution.

## Verified criteria

The final rules specify these weights. [Official event](https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon), [final rules](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to demonstrate |
|---|---:|---|
| Impact | 30% | A person can state a condition, distinguish support from missing evidence, and leave with a useful next check |
| Use of Meta AI Tooling | 25% | An actual model-to-tool exchange that clarifies intent or retrieves scoped evidence, with the real trace |
| Technical Depth and Execution | 20% | Complete-source acquisition, native identity, geometry/QC guards, reporting hierarchy, paired weather, provenance and reproducibility |
| Demo | 25% | One understandable journey through three consequential reveals to an inspectable receipt on the final tested build |

Measured time savings, financial returns, decision accuracy and business outcomes have not been established. Impact claims should describe the demonstrated workflow until user research supplies stronger evidence.

## One question, three reveals, one next step

Start with **Understand a place**, **Compare addresses** or **Find candidates**. Preserve that general scope. A warehouse or cold-storage purpose can suggest follow-up questions; it does not silently add conditions or certify a use. Show a real place promptly, then ask what it must support.

A demonstration condition such as a minimum mapped footprint can be evaluated from public geometry. A condition for available electrical capacity needs different evidence. Keep the required measurement, unit and original condition visible; do not substitute roof height for clear height, total tax-lot floor area for usable space, or annual consumption for available capacity.

**Reveal the reporting boundary.** At 211 Lombardy Street, native parent and child reports repeat an annual energy total but use different floor-area denominators. The displayed EUI changes from 66.7 to 127.6. The useful next request is a meter-to-building schedule, the reporting area basis and the proposed operating use. Do not infer efficiency, shared physical metering, an error or savings, and never add parent and child totals.

**Reveal what the map leaves out.** At 572 and 592 Smith Street, DEP's surface-water model treats buildings as voids. A blank interior is therefore not a safety finding. Read the surrounding ground under the pinned rainfall and sea-level assumptions, and investigate actual entrances, loading areas, drainage and equipment elevations. An outside-outline band is a chosen geometric search distance, not an official risk threshold or surveyed accessible ground. The model does not establish indoor depth, damage, safe access or annual probability.

**Reveal the timestamp.** JFK's hottest retained 2025 sample and highest-dew-point sample occurred at different times. Keep each actual temperature/dew-point pair together, with its source timestamp, ties and missing-hour coverage. An independent pair of maxima would create conditions not observed in the retained sample. The useful next question concerns required indoor conditions and documented system performance. Airport observations are not building measurements or engineering design weather.

The examples describe different locations and scopes. Present them as a common way to investigate evidence, never as interchangeable measurements of one property. Daily station history and the separate NPCC4 regional outlook add context with their own periods and baselines. They cannot produce invented building temperatures, cooling capacity, losses or a hidden suitability score.

Finish with the deterministic evidence receipt: question, discovery filters, explicit conditions, native IDs, public facts, separately attributed user reports, sources and the next unresolved check. A saved report is attributable; it is not independently verified merely because the user entered it.

## The prepared Meta role and the remaining gap

**Meta API approval is pending. The key is unconnected. No actual provider response or model-driven tool sequence has been verified.** The latest participant statement governs this status; earlier planning assumptions about access are superseded. Event participation and API approval are separate.

The prepared server uses Meta Chat Completions to choose a consequential clarification, permitted official-data retrieval or typed plan. The application validates measurements, scopes, native IDs and user quotations, then computes the conditional answer. It does not accept invented operational observations or free-form model prose as a completed factual assessment. A clarification may finish without retrieval.

Two scoped investigations are also prepared: bounded native LL84 reporting-scope queries, and compact climate snapshots containing daily/period statistics, published NPCC tables or a same-time airport pair. The selected source, station, year or horizon stays reviewable. The chat route does not retrieve the full-source surroundings archive; that remains a separate guided public-data investigation. Point context is not a substitute for outside-outline analysis.

The endpoint is `https://api.meta.ai/v1/chat/completions` with server-side Bearer authentication. The prepared model name is `muse-spark-1.3`; actual account/model availability still requires verification. Supported tool choice is `auto`, not `required`; each returned tool-call ID must match its real result. [Chat Completions](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/protocols/chat-completions), [tool calling](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/tool-calling).

Calls remain blocked by default. After access is granted and the participant authorizes connection, configure the server-only credential and explicit approval flag, then verify actual authentication, clarification, source retrieval and the displayed result. A configured secret, controlled mock test or direct public-data check is not proof of live Meta execution. This is a material gap for the 25% tooling criterion; do not disguise it with a scripted response.

## What the demo must prove

Use the current three-reveal narration in `submission/demo-script.txt` as a rehearsal target; its 90-second length is an editorial choice, not an organizer rule. A coherent sequence is more useful than a tour of layers. The 3D atlas and common-scale comparison outlines should clarify physical records; they are not a surveyed digital twin, interior volume or proof of usability.

The preserved Brooklyn examples are public records, not verified listings or validated cold-storage sites. Searches return bounded official-source pages, not a citywide optimum. The complete source archive is separate from the browser products and deployed queries. Retain each record's snapshot/query mode and date; never relabel a preserved example as a live observation.

Show source failure and unknown states honestly. Demonstrate only functions that work on the final build. The receipt, source links, return navigation and mobile reading need their own final checks. Implementation and earlier tests do not establish a successful deployment.
